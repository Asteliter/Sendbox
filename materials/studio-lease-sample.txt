COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT ("Lease") is made and entered into as of August 1, 2026, by and between RIVERSIDE PROPERTIES LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned tenant ("Tenant"), for the premises located at 118 Harrow Street, Unit 4B (the "Premises").

1. TERM
The initial term of this Lease shall be one (1) year, commencing September 1, 2026 and ending August 31, 2027 ("Initial Term").

2. RENT
Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,400, due on the first day of each month. A late fee of $75 applies to payments received after the 5th of the month.

3. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used solely as an art and design studio and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

4. RENEWAL
This Lease shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms upon the same terms and conditions unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal no less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Failure to provide timely notice shall obligate Tenant to the full rent and terms of the renewal term regardless of Tenant's intent to vacate.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit $2,400 as security, refundable within 30 days of move-out less any deductions for damages, cleaning, or unpaid rent.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant shall be responsible for all damage to the Premises occurring during the Lease term, regardless of cause, and shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims, losses, or expenses arising out of Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises, including claims arising from the acts or omissions of third parties, Landlord, or Landlord's agents.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
Should Tenant terminate this Lease prior to the end of the then-current term for any reason, Tenant shall owe Landlord an early termination fee equal to three (3) months' rent, in addition to rent accrued through the date of termination, which sum shall be immediately due and payable.

8. ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered under the rules then in effect, and each party waives any right to a jury trial or to participate in a class action.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises are located.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first written above.
